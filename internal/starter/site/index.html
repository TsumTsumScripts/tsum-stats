First, fetch Google's adb

              How updating works
              
The starter is this folder's scripts; tsum-stats serves this page and the Stats site.
                Updating downloads the new version from GitHub, checks it against the published checksum and restarts
                the starter in this window; the page reloads by itself. Your adb, APKs, copied files and settings stay
                as they are. It also looks every few hours while open.

              What watch mode does
              
    Checks this device every 15 seconds. It starts GAP after the device
                restarts, restarts GAP when it stops answering, and restarts a frozen emulator. GAP can also restart the
                device and carry on afterwards.
    
              
    
              
    Watch mode runs inside this program: keep this window and its terminal open, or it stops.
                Its limits are in watch.json in this folder, and what it did in
                watch.log.

    Help
    getting a device listed · when something is wrong · stats    
          
    
            
    
              
    Getting a device listed
    
              
      
                
    • Start your emulator, or plug the phone in with USB debugging on: Settings > About phone, tap
                  Build number seven times, then Developer options > USB debugging.
      • 
                
    • The first time, the phone asks Allow USB debugging? Tap Allow, then Refresh.
      • 
                
    • A device you expect is missing, or stuck offline? Restart adb restarts the adb server and
                  looks again.
      • 
                
    • MuMu, LDPlayer, Nox and MEmu are found on their usual ports, four instances each. Another port: set
                  GAP_EXTRA_PORTS="12345" before starting.
      • 
              
    
            
    
            
    
              
    When something is wrong
    
              
      
                
    • wrong ABI: the installed app was built for another processor. Install the latest APK; it
                  fetches the build for this device.
      • 
                
    • started by the app itself: a rooted device. The app manages the service; after updating the app,
                  reboot the device.
      • 
                
    • Replaced the ADB server: harmless. Android Studio or your emulator manager reconnects.
      • 
                
    • Watch mode keeps GAP running while this program runs. It restarts MuMu Player 12, LDPlayer 9
                  and BlueStacks 5 on Windows, and BlueStacks Air on a Mac; elsewhere it uses adb only.
      • 
                
    • Stuck? Export logs & stats and share the zip.
      • 
              
    
            
    
            
    
              
    Stats
    
              
    The Stats site runs here too, at the same
                address. Import round stats into Stats copies a device's stats_*.csv and Tsum lists
                into it; the app can also send rounds live (Settings > Script events).
    
              
    Stop at the top of the page stops both, and closes the starter's terminal window.
